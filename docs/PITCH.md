# Pitch

Nimbus Nova is a short adult task demo. Andrei publishes a two-hour public setup job for 100.00 RON proposed. Maria applies from the phone. Andrei accepts on the website. Maria sees the task assigned. Andrei marks it finished. Nobody is paid and nobody is employed.

Show the live state change, the two seeded roles, and the safety limits: no driving, no home access, no cash, no one under 18 in paid work. Do not claim Glovo, Tazz, Uber, Lidl, a Nova employment contract, a diploma, or legal compliance.

The website is the poster. The phone is the worker. Admin is `/admin` and can hide a task or reset the demo. Identity capture exists, but face matching is not available, so verified signup stays blocked.
